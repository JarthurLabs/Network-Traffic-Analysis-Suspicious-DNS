# Realism Review Notes

## What sounded too AI-generated before

The repo had strong artifacts, but the writing made the investigation sound too complete. It risked implying that suspicious DNS automatically meant compromise.

The earlier wording also made screenshots and queries feel like portfolio decorations rather than part of the investigative workflow.

## How this version was improved

- The README now starts with the actual analyst thought process.
- The conclusion is more cautious.
- The PCAP, tcpdump output, screenshots, and filters are tied directly to the workflow.
- False positives are treated as part of the investigation, not an afterthought.
- The project now clearly says what could not be confirmed without endpoint telemetry.
